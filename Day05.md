# Day05


Key Responsibilities of cloud security Engineer

*Cloud Security Engineers Work across people, process and technology to build secure cloud environments.

What They do (High Level)

01. **Identity & Access Management** Manage users, roles and permissions with least privilege.
02. **Network Security** Secure VPCs, security groups, firewalls and connections.
03. **Data Protection** Encrypt data at rest and in transit and manage keys.
04. **Monitoring & Logging** Monitor activity, analyze logs and detect threats.
05. **Threat Detection & Response** Identify threats early and respond to security incidents quickly.
06. **Compliance & Governance** Ensure policies, standards and audits are followed.
07. **Automation** Automate security checks and enforce best practices.
08. **Vulnerability Management** Scan, assess and fix vulnerabilities regularly.
09. **Secure Configurations** Follow secure baselines and harden cloud resources.
10. **Incident Reporting** Document incidents and improve security continuously.

**Their goal is simple `Prevent attacks`, `protects data` and keep the cloud environment `secure and reliable.`**
